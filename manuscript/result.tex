\section{Results}

Table~\ref{tab:summary_metrics} reports the mean segmentation performance across the four benchmark histological images for all four methods. VeSpA achieved the best mean Dice coefficient (0.7433) and IoU (0.5929). The hybrid VeSpA-SAM method ranked second with a mean Dice coefficient of 0.6979 and the highest mean precision (0.8739), indicating that stain-guided prompting substantially reduced false positives relative to the generic SAM baseline. However, the hybrid method remained slightly below VeSpA in overlap-based metrics because its recall (0.5958) was lower than VeSpA's 0.7052.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Mean performance metrics computed from \texttt{results/metrics/results\_all\_categories.csv}.}
\label{tab:summary_metrics}
\begin{tabular}{lcccc}
\hline
Model & Dice & IoU & Precision & Recall \\
\hline
VeSpA & 0.7433 & 0.5929 & 0.8059 & 0.7052 \\
VeSpA+SAM (Hybrid) & 0.6979 & 0.5397 & 0.8739 & 0.5958 \\
SAM & 0.2988 & 0.1782 & 0.6403 & 0.1968 \\
YOLOv8-seg & 0.1263 & 0.0711 & 0.7557 & 0.0736 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Per-image results are shown in Table~\ref{tab:per_image_results}. The hybrid method produced substantial gains over automatic SAM on all four images, raising Dice from 0.3388 to 0.5952 on \texttt{ROI\_1}, from 0.1607 to 0.7229 on \texttt{ROI\_2}, from 0.3459 to 0.7247 on \texttt{ROI\_3}, and from 0.3497 to 0.7487 on \texttt{ROI\_4}. Despite these improvements, VeSpA remained the top-performing method on every image in this benchmark.

Runtime measurements are summarized in Table~\ref{tab:runtime}. YOLOv8-seg remained the fastest method at 0.63 s per image, followed by VeSpA at 2.58 s. The hybrid VeSpA-SAM method required 43.46 s per image on average, making it the slowest configuration in the current repository benchmark because it runs prompt-guided SAM refinement over multiple stain-derived components per image.

VeSpA & 2.58 \\

\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The plots confirm that the hybrid pipeline closes much of the gap between baseline SAM and VeSpA while clearly outperforming YOLOv8-seg in overlap-based metrics. Qualitative overlays saved under \path{results/prediction/} show that the hybrid method is more conservative than VeSpA, with higher precision but lower recall on several images.
